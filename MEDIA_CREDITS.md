Matching frames are optimized local WebP posters. Neither format contains an audio stream. Modern browsers select one supported format, never both. Originals were processed outside the repository.

The shared background component starts media near the viewport and pauses it offscreen. Reduced-motion and data-saving preferences show the poster without requesting the movie. A visible pause/play control supports manual motion control. Failed or blocked playback leaves the local poster readable beneath the approved gradients.
